# Nimbus Cloud Storage — Internal Support & IT Policy Handbook
**Version 2.0 — Effective Date: April 1, 2026**
**Supersedes Version 1.0. Changed sections are marked with [UPDATED].**

This handbook governs internal IT policies and customer-support procedures for Nimbus Cloud Storage employees. All support staff must follow these policies when assisting customers or configuring internal systems.

## Section 1: Account Security Policy

### 1.1 Password Reset Frequency [UPDATED]
All employee passwords must be reset every **60 days**. The system will send an automated reminder 7 days before expiration. Passwords that are not reset within the grace period will result in account lockout.

### 1.2 Two-Factor Authentication [UPDATED]
Two-factor authentication (2FA) is now **mandatory for all employee accounts**, including standard accounts, following the Q1 security audit.

### 1.3 VPN Access
Remote VPN access requires a session timeout of **8 hours**, after which employees must re-authenticate.

## Section 2: Customer Refund Policy

### 2.1 Standard Refund Window [UPDATED]
Customers may request a full refund within **45 days** of purchase for any Nimbus storage plan, provided the account has not exceeded 10% of its storage quota.

### 2.2 Refund Processing Time
Approved refunds are processed within **5 business days** and returned to the customer's original payment method.

## Section 3: Data Retention & Backup Policy

### 3.1 Backup Retention Period [UPDATED]
Customer data backups are retained for **90 days** after account deletion, after which they are permanently purged from Nimbus servers.

### 3.2 Backup Frequency
Automated backups occur **once every 24 hours** for all paid-tier accounts. Free-tier accounts are not backed up automatically.

## Section 4: Customer Support SLA

### 4.1 Response Time — Standard Tier
Standard-tier customers can expect an initial support response within **24 hours** of ticket submission.

### 4.2 Response Time — Priority Tier [UPDATED]
Priority-tier (paid) customers can expect an initial support response within **2 hours** of ticket submission, following the December support-team expansion.

### 4.3 Escalation Path
Unresolved tickets are automatically escalated to a Tier 2 specialist after **48 hours** without resolution.

## Section 5: Storage Plan Specifications

### 5.1 Basic Plan [UPDATED]
The Basic Plan includes **150 GB** of storage for **$4.99/month**.

### 5.2 Pro Plan
The Pro Plan includes **2 TB** of storage for **$12.99/month**.

### 5.3 Team Plan
The Team Plan includes **5 TB** of shared storage for up to 10 users, priced at **$39.99/month**.
